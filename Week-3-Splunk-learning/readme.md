## Week 3 – Introduction to Splunk

### Objective

The objective of Week 3 is to gain foundational knowledge of **Splunk**, one of the leading platforms used for security information and event management (SIEM).

Through the **Introduction to Splunk** training and lab activities, I developed an understanding of how security professionals use Splunk to collect, search, analyse, and visualise machine-generated data. These skills are essential for cybersecurity roles, particularly within a **Security Operations Centre (SOC)**.

---

## Why Learn Splunk?

Modern organisations generate millions of log events every day from a wide range of sources, including:

* Computers and endpoints
* Servers
* Firewalls
* Applications
* Cloud services
* Network devices

Manually reviewing all these logs would be extremely time-consuming and inefficient.

**Splunk** helps security and IT teams collect, organise, search, and analyse machine-generated data from different sources. It can help analysts identify suspicious activity, investigate security incidents, monitor systems, and respond to potential cyber threats.

Splunk is commonly used by:

* Security Operations Centres (SOC)
* Incident Response Teams
* Cybersecurity Analysts
* Threat Hunters
* IT Operations Teams

Learning Splunk provides valuable hands-on experience with a widely used cybersecurity and IT operations platform.
